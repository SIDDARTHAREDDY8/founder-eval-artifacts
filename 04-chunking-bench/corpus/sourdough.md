# Sourdough starter guide

## Feeding schedule
Feed the starter twice a day at room temperature. Use a 1:2:2 ratio of
starter to flour to water by weight. A healthy starter doubles in four to
eight hours after feeding. If it smells like acetone, it is hungry: feed it.

## Flour choice
Bread flour gives the strongest gluten network. Whole wheat ferments faster
because of higher enzyme activity. A 50/50 blend is a good default for
beginners. Avoid bleached flour: the bleaching process harms wild yeast.

## Baking day timeline
Mix the levain the night before. Autolyse flour and water for one hour, then
add starter and salt. Bulk ferment four to six hours with folds every 45
minutes. Shape, cold-proof overnight at 4C, then bake in a Dutch oven at
250C for 20 minutes covered and 20 uncovered.

## Troubleshooting
Dense crumb usually means under-fermentation: extend the bulk. A pale crust
means the oven was too cool or the bake too short. If the loaf spreads flat,
the shaping lacked tension or the proof ran too long.
