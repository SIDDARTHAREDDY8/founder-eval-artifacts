# Bicycle brake maintenance

## Pad inspection
Check brake pads every 500 km. Replace pads when the wear groove disappears.
Contaminated pads squeal and lose stopping power; sand them lightly or
replace them. Keep oil and chain lube away from the rotor.

## Hydraulic disc brakes
Bleed hydraulic brakes once a year or when the lever feels spongy. Use the
brake fluid the manufacturer specifies: DOT and mineral oil are not
interchangeable. A proper bleed removes air bubbles that compress under
pressure and cause fade on long descents.

## Cable brakes
Replace inner cables yearly; frayed cables snap without warning. Set pad
clearance to about 1 mm per side. Lubricate the cable lightly where it runs
through housing, but never near the pads.

## Rotor care
Clean rotors with isopropyl alcohol only. A bent rotor rubs once per
revolution: true it gently with an adjustable wrench. Minimum rotor
thickness is usually 1.5 mm; measure with calipers.
